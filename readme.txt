The first 25 percent of a private, American English worldwide clinic and appointment-request system.

== Features ==
* Public clinic browsing without registration.
* Login required for appointment requests.
* Verified doctor availability with days, hours, time zone, duration, consultation type, and temporary unavailability.
* Private patient and doctor dashboards.
* Requested, under-review, accepted, reschedule-requested, declined, cancelled, and completed statuses.
* Phone and WhatsApp contact buttons inside authorized dashboards.
* UTC storage with patient and doctor time-zone display.
* Email status notifications without sensitive medical details.
* Clinic Management, doctor assignment, filters, settings, and audit history.
* Emergency warnings, consent, rate limits, noindex private pages, and privacy callbacks.

== Requirements ==
Files 03 and 07 must be active. Keep Files 01 through 07 active for complete platform integration.

== Installation ==
1. Upload the ZIP through WordPress Admin > Plugins > Add New > Upload Plugin.
2. Activate Worldwide Clinic and Appointments Foundation.
3. Configure Clinic Management > Clinic Settings.
4. Ask verified doctors to configure Doctor Availability.
5. Test one appointment with separate patient and doctor accounts before public launch.

== Important limitation ==
This foundation does not provide emergency care, payments, video visits, prescriptions, medical record uploads, diagnosis, ratings, or automated medical advice.
